Yahoo Customer Care

Agent Role: Technical Support & Account Security

1. Opening Script

"Thank you for calling Yahoo Support. My name is [Your Name]. May I start with your first and last name, please?"

Confirm spelling of the name.
Ask for the Yahoo email address in question.
2. Verification (Security First)
REQUIRED

"For your security, I’ve sent a verification code to your recovery [phone/email]. Could you please read that back to me?"

Pro-Tip: If they cannot access recovery info, pivot to Account Recovery Specialist transfer or the Sign-in Helper tool.
3. Empathy & Discovery

"I understand how important it is to get back into your account. Can you tell me exactly what happens when you try to sign in?"

Common Issues:
  • Forgotten Password
  • Account Locked (Too many attempts)
  • Spam/Hacking concerns
  • Yahoo Mail Sync (Mobile App)
4. The Solution

"I am going to guide you through the [Procedure Name] now. Please keep the window open while we complete this..."

5. Closing & Survey

"Is there anything else I can assist you with today, [Customer Name]? ... Thank you for choosing Yahoo. You may receive a short survey regarding my performance; we’d love to hear your feedback. Have a great day!"
