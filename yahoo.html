1 Opening & Introduction

"Thank you for calling Yahoo! support. My name is [Agent Name]. I understand you need help unlocking your email account—I'll be happy to assist!
May I have your full name and the email address you’re calling about?"

2 Identity Verification In Progress

"For your security, I’ll need to verify a few details on your account."

  • Only proceed if all verification items are completed and caller matches account info.
  • If verification fails, pause and escalate per policy.
  • Never share reset links or PINs if caller fails verification.
  • Be patient and assure the caller: "This is for your protection."
3 Issue Discovery

"Thanks for confirming. I see your account is locked due to multiple unsuccessful sign-in attempts.
Can you tell me if you remember your current password or if you recently changed devices?"

Recent Account Events:
  • 5 failed logins (mobile app, 1 hour ago)
  • Login from new device detected
  • Password unchanged in 6 months
Tip: Be empathetic; frustration is common after lockouts.
