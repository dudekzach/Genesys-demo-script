Yahoo Support Interaction Script

1. Opening

"Thank you for contacting Yahoo Support, this is [Agent Name]. May I have your full name and the email address you're calling about today?"

2. Verification

"To keep your account secure, I’ll need to verify some information."

3. Discovery

"Thanks for confirming your identity. Can you describe what happens when you try to sign in?"

4. Resolution

"I'm going to try and unlock the account from our side. This will take a few moments."

  • ✔ Verified caller identity
  • ✔ Retrieved account lock reason
  • ✔ Sent unlock token to recovery email
  • ✔ Confirmed token delivery and login success

5. Confirmation

"Can you please confirm that you're now able to sign in?"

Customer recently traveled. Login attempts from new IP flagged as suspicious. Frequent password resets noted.

6. Wrap-Up

"I’m glad we got you back into your account. Is there anything else I can assist you with today?"

  • ✅ Verified 2+ pieces of customer information
  • ✅ No PII repeated back unnecessarily
  • ✅ Clear confirmation of successful outcome
  • ✅ Offered follow-up assistance

Unlock Failed

We encountered an error unlocking this account. Please escalate to Tier 2 support.
