Yahoo! Locked Out Email Account — Agent Script

Customer Context

The caller is a Yahoo! email user who is locked out of their email account and contacting support for help restoring access.

Scenario Overview

Guide the caller through verification, account recovery steps, and resolution of their locked email account.

Preparation

Ensure you have access to Yahoo’s internal recovery tools and verification prompts before connecting.

Agent Script Flow

SYSTEM: Begin call and display caller’s partial info.

AGENT: “Thank you for contacting Yahoo! Support. My name is ________. May I have your full name, please?”

AGENT: “To help you regain access to your email, I need to verify your identity. Can you please confirm your recovery email or phone on file?”

CUSTOMER: “________”

AGENT: “Thank you. Next, I’m going to ask a few questions about your account usage to verify it’s really you.”

  • Recent email subject line you sent
  • Date of last successful login
  • Two-factor code if enabled

AGENT: “I’m now initiating the account recovery flow. Please stay on the line.”

Branches

Account Recovered Successfully

AGENT: “Great! Your identity is verified, and I’ve reset your access. Are you able to log in now?”

Verification Failed / Escalate to Supervisor

AGENT: “I’m sorry, but I couldn’t verify your account with the details provided. I will escalate this to a supervisor who can assist further.”

Data Capture Table

FieldEntry ControlPurpose
Full Name Identify caller
Recovery Email/Phone Verification
Verification Answers Proof of ownership

Compliance Notes

Ensure you follow all privacy and security protocols before accessing or resetting account details.
