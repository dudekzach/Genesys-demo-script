1stSourceBank — Fraudulent Transaction Activity Verification

Version: 1.0 | Date: 01/08/2026 | Channel: Branch Teller | Duration: ~10–15 min

Greeting Verification Transaction Review Report Fraud Confirm & Wrap-Up After Interaction

Greeting

Agent: “Good morning/afternoon, thank you for calling 1stSourceBank. My name is [Your Name]. How can I assist you today?”

Verification

Purpose: Confirm identity before accessing account details.

Agent: “Before we review your account, can I please have your full name, date of birth, and the last four of your SSN?”

Agent: “For security, can you confirm your current mailing address?”

Review Recent Transactions

Agent: “Thank you. I will look up your recent transactions. Can you tell me which charges you are questioning?”

Display account transactions and ask:

  • “I see these charges — are these the ones you do not recognize?”
  • “Were any of these authorized by you?”

Report Fraudulent Charges

Agent: “Thanks for pointing those out. Let’s report the ones you believe are fraudulent.”

Action: Mark selected transactions as disputed in system and create a fraud report.

Common Exception: Customer missing card but transactions are expected

“I understand the card was lost, but did you authorize any transactions before reporting it lost?”

If yes: confirm and only flag unauthorized ones.

Alternate: Customer unsure about a merchant name

“Sometimes merchant names differ. Could this be a name you recognize on your statement?”

Confirmation and Customer Wrap-Up

Agent: “I’ve submitted the dispute for these transactions. You will receive confirmation and next steps via your preferred contact method.”

Agent: “Is there anything else I can help you with today?”
