📝 Quick Notes (This Interaction)
📋 Add Teen Driver — Step Checklist
  • Greet caller & confirm intent to add new driver Verify this is a voluntary add, not a state-required notice.
  • Authenticate policyholder identity (full name + policy # OR full name + DOB + address) Follow two-factor verification protocol.
  • Collect teen driver details: full name, DOB, license number, license state, issue date License must be a valid, non-learner's-permit license in most states.
  • Identify primary vehicle assignment for teen driver Teen is typically rated on the highest-risk or lowest-premium vehicle — confirm with rating system.
  • Check and offer Good Student Discount GPA ≥ 3.0 or top 20% of class. Proof required (transcript or report card).
  • Check and offer Teen Safe Driver (Telematics/KnowYourDrive) enrollment Offer potential premium discount for monitored driving behavior.
  • Provide updated premium estimate and obtain verbal confirmation to proceed Recite new monthly and/or 6-month premium. Document verbal consent.
  • Process endorsement in policy management system Confirm effective date — typically same-day or next day for new drivers.
  • Confirm updated declarations page and ID cards will be sent Digital delivery if ePolicy enrolled; paper otherwise.
  • Wrap-up: summarize changes, thank customer, complete after-call work
